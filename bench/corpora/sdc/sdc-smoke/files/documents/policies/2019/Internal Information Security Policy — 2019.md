# Responsibilities

Internal standard code: POL-2019-0036.

Digital records are retained for seven years unless a different legal period applies.

Disposal requires dual confirmation and produces an immutable audit record.

Backups are verified monthly through a restore test.

# Information classification

Information is classified as public, internal, confidential, or restricted according to the impact of improper disclosure.

- Test continuity plans at least once a year.

- Delete temporary copies after the final record is validated.
