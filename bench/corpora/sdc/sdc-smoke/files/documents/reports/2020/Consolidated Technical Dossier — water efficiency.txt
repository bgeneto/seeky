General context

Annex B describes the data model and the validation rules applied.

The equipment inventory was reconciled with accounting records at the end of the third quarter.

Measurement uncertainty was estimated from three independent replicates per point.

The risk analysis considers equipment failure, supplier unavailability, and extreme weather events.

Data collection ran in weekly cycles with automatic validation for missing values and out-of-range readings.

The communication plan provides monthly summary reports and one consolidated annual report.

Availability indicators stayed above the contractual threshold in every month analysed.

Future work includes extending the perimeter to two new sites and automating inventory reconciliation.

The preventive maintenance plan was completed for ninety-four percent of scheduled interventions.

Each cycle produces an immutable record that is compared with the previous cycle to detect abrupt changes.

Year-on-year comparison should be read with care because the measurement perimeter changed.

Variation between sites is explained mainly by differences in size and intensity of use.

Results by domain are presented in normalised tables to allow direct comparison.

The conclusions highlight the overall stability of indicators and the need for continued monitoring.

Annex C presents the complete time series by domain and by site.

Specific consumption per unit area fell consistently over the period.

Conclusions

Annex A lists the internal codes used throughout the document.

The technical team documented every intervention, including component replacements and configuration adjustments.

Local team training was reinforced with practical sessions on data recording and incident response.

Corrective actions were recorded with a deadline, an owner, and completion evidence.

Supporting documentation includes procedures, diagrams, and checklists used in the field.

Sensitivity analysis shows that the results are robust to small changes in calibration parameters.

All entities mentioned are fictitious and the values exist only for search-engine evaluation.

Improvement opportunities were identified in the calibration of temperature and humidity sensors.

The assessed perimeter covers seven sites across three districts, all identified by internal codes with no reference to individuals.

For each risk, the team defined a mitigation measure, a tracking indicator, and a review interval.

The monitoring system recorded three scheduled outages and no unplanned outage.
