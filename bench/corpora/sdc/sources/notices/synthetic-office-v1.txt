======================================

Source set: synthetic-office-v1
Redistribution class: permissive-core

The documents in this source set are synthetic templates authored for the
Seeky Desktop Corpus. They contain no personal data, real contact details, or
third-party text. Every company, person role, identifier, address, and date is
fictitious, and every e-mail address uses a reserved example domain.
